## Moderator script

1. Confirm consent and remind the participant that participation is voluntary and can stop at any time.
2. Say: “We are looking at an illustrative prototype with fixture content. It cannot sign you in, save anything, connect an Agent, or access real research. We are evaluating the design, not your ability.”
3. Ask the participant not to enter private, confidential, or credential information. Ask them to think aloud and explain expectations in their own words.
4. Present the A09 tasks one at a time. Read each verbatim and avoid leading the participant toward a particular label.
5. For the keyboard task, ensure the participant can opt out if keyboard use is not comfortable; record that as an accommodation, not a failure.
6. After all tasks, ask: “What felt clear? What felt uncertain? What would you expect before using this in real research work?”
7. Thank the participant, explain how to withdraw their data where applicable, and record only de-identified notes in the approved study system.
