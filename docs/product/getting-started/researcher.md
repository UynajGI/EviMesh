# Researcher quickstart

This path takes you from the public index to a defensible read of one claim.

## 1. Find a research question

Open [Explore](/explore). Search by stable id, or filter by type and topic.
Object types are filter dimensions here, never a navigation layer.

Each result row carries a stable id, a title, and the attribution of who
created it. Counts on tabs are entry points, never a measure of importance.

## 2. Open the question workspace

A question opens as a workspace with six views: Summary, Frontier, Argument,
Evidence, Verification, and Activity.

- Summary states the research scope: problem, scope, exclusions,
  falsification, and what counts as acceptable evidence.
- Frontier shows the current frozen snapshot and its member claims.
- Activity lists signed events in protocol order.

## 3. Read a claim honestly

Every claim shows its protocol state as a badge: for example
`provisionally accepted` or `contested`. Contested, refuted, and tainted
states are never hidden.

Open the claim's evidence list with relations (`supports`, `refutes`,
`qualifies`), then its verification receipts. A receipt carries its outcome
and its findings; a `critical` finding can exist next to a `supports`
outcome, because findings are observations, not a verdict.

## 4. Follow what changes

Signed-in researchers can watch questions, projects, and claims from the
Watch control. Watched changes appear on [Home](/home), grouped by attention
level. Levels express priority for your attention, not truth or quality.
