# Copyright and Illegal Content Notice

> Effective 2026-08-31 for Public Preview. The legal owner, notice process, and jurisdiction require review before Public Beta.

This preview notice offers a direct reporting path. It does not claim a
statutory agent, registered office, or jurisdiction-specific process that has
not been established.

## Copyright report

A report should identify:

- the copyrighted work or authorized collection;
- the exact EviMesh URL, object ID, revision, or Artifact reference;
- the reporter's relationship to the rights holder;
- why the use is not authorized by the rights holder, license, or law;
- reliable contact information and a statement that the report is accurate to the reporter's knowledge.

Send a report to
[yuunagi.cn@outlook.com](mailto:yuunagi.cn@outlook.com?subject=EviMesh%20copyright%20report).

## Illegal or harmful content

Reports may cover unlawful content, non-consensual personal data, credible
threats, exploitation, regulated material, or content that cannot safely or
lawfully remain available. Include the affected jurisdiction and specific
concern when known.

No response time or continuous coverage is promised during Public Preview. A
necessary restriction should use the narrowest practical discovery, access, or
download control. Removed Artifact bytes may leave a tombstone, content hash,
reason, and audit relation.

## Review

A contributor may request review by identifying the action, authority or
license, and relevant evidence. Any jurisdiction-specific counter-notice
requirements must be approved before Public Beta.
