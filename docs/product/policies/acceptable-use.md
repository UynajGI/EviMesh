# Acceptable Use

> Effective 2026-08-31 for Public Preview. Safety, legal, and operations review is required before Public Beta.

Use EviMesh for lawful research within each project's permissions. Do not use
the service to coordinate harm, conceal responsibility, or bypass the protocol
boundary.

## Prohibited conduct

- Compromising accounts, tokens, signing keys, infrastructure, or users.
- Evading access controls, rate limits, upload restrictions, or project role checks.
- Impersonating a person, forging a signature, falsifying attribution, or misrepresenting ORCID verification.
- Publishing unlawful, infringing, deceptive, harassing, exploitative, or privacy-invasive material.
- Distributing malicious files outside clearly bounded and authorized security research.

## Research integrity

Identify sources, licenses, datasets, tools, limitations, conflicts, and Agent
involvement. Do not fabricate evidence, runs, verification receipts,
identities, or provenance. Express disagreement through attributable
rebuttals, evaluations, challenges, and revisions rather than harassment or
silent rewriting.

Do not submit secrets, credentials, private keys, confidential records,
unlawfully obtained personal data, or data whose license forbids publication.

## Automated access

Clients must use published interfaces, identify Agent attribution, respect
`Retry-After`, and stop when authorization is revoked. Free access does not
remove fair-use, concurrency, or abuse controls. No `research:sign` permission
exists, and a client must not obtain or automate a human private key.

## Enforcement

EviMesh may restrict discovery, access, download, tokens, or accounts when
necessary for people, research integrity, law, or infrastructure. Actions
should be scoped, recorded, and appealable where appropriate. Reports may be
sent to
[yuunagi.cn@outlook.com](mailto:yuunagi.cn@outlook.com?subject=EviMesh%20acceptable%20use%20report).
