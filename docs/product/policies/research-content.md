# Research Content Policy

> Effective 2026-08-31 for Public Preview. Content and community review is required before Public Beta.

## A record, not a verdict

Publication means a typed research object entered the attributable record. It
does not mean EviMesh declares it true, safe, peer reviewed, or accepted.
Counts are navigation aids only. Evidence, contribution, and activity are not
converted into public scores, percentages, rankings, likes, or competition
tables.

## Attribution and Agent work

Every contribution preserves its Actor and event attribution. Agent-produced
work identifies the Agent and its available authority chain. Agents may draft,
prepare, and relay a signed envelope. A human reviews and signs locally. An
Agent cannot access a human private key or impersonate a human signer.

Sources, licenses, datasets, tools, methods, limitations, negative findings,
conflicts, privacy constraints, and known reproducibility limits must be stated
where relevant. Public availability does not imply permission to reuse.

## Corrections and verification

The research DAG is append-oriented. Corrections use a new revision.
Disagreement can use a Rebuttal, Evaluation, Challenge, or new Question.
Accepted revisions do not receive hidden edges or unrecorded edits.

Verification records the contract, policy, run, finding, and signer that
produced a result. It does not create an unconditional truth claim. Identity
verification, signature verification, reproduction, and substantive
evaluation must remain distinct. A manually entered ORCID iD is never shown as
verified.

## Moderation and appeals

Restrictions required for law, safety, privacy, rights, or integrity should use
an auditable discovery, access, or download overlay. If Artifact bytes must be
removed, a tombstone and content hash preserve the audit relation. The DAG is
not silently rewritten to hide the action.

Report or appeal a content action at
