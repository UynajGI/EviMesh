# Terms

> Effective 2026-08-31 for Public Preview. Legal and Release Lead review is required before Public Beta.

EviMesh is a Public Preview. Features may be incomplete, unavailable, or
changed as safety and protocol work continues. The preview is not intended for
emergency, clinical, legal, financial, or other safety-critical decisions.

## Accounts and authority

- Provide accurate account information and protect authentication methods, tokens, and local signing keys.
- Use an account or organization only when authorized.
- A project role controls research contribution rights. An account alone does not authorize a submission.
- A verified external identity is not an endorsement of a person or their research.

## Contributions

The website is a reading surface for research. Writing follows draft, prepare,
human local signature, and submit through supported clients. The signer is
responsible for reviewing the canonical envelope.

Contributors retain rights they already hold and grant the permissions needed
to store, display, index, validate, and distribute a contribution as part of
EviMesh. Broader reuse follows the license attached to the relevant object or
Artifact. Contributors must have permission to share material and must state
licenses, provenance, restrictions, Agent involvement, and material
limitations accurately.

## Record and moderation

Corrections, rebuttals, challenges, and evaluations normally become new
attributable objects or revisions. Accepted history is not silently rewritten.
A platform state or verification record is not a declaration of truth, peer
review, professional advice, or endorsement. EviMesh publishes no competitive
scores or rankings.

Access may be limited for law, safety, privacy, rights, security, or
infrastructure protection. Actions should use narrow, auditable overlays. An
Artifact may be tombstoned without falsifying the historical hash relation.

## Preview availability

The service is provided as available during Public Preview without a promise
of uninterrupted operation, data permanence, fitness for a purpose, or
accuracy of contributed research. Governing law, jurisdiction, mandatory
consumer language, and liability wording require pre-Beta legal approval.
