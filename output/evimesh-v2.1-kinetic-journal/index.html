Do small language models preserve calibration after tool use?

PUBLISHED

30 AUG 2026

PRIMARY ANSWERans_2PX4 / R02

Calibration shifts are concentrated in unobserved tool failures.

Across three model families, expected calibration error remained stable when tool results were observed. The measurable shift appeared after silent execution failures and narrowed after failure-aware decoding.

STATUSACTIVEFALSIFIES IF

The effect persists after execution state is revealed and temperature is controlled.
